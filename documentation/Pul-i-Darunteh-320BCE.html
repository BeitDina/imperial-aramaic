Pul-i-DArunteh - Aegeon of Syracuse

Line

Original (Aramaic script)

Romanian Transliteration

English translation

1


צמ גע

... ţem gheo...

Made (by) Geo

2

ואוכ לאנש

...uî-ekâ lî'enaş...

And idem to man!

3


...uî-dîi-nîz-Iţi Neo...

And to me Iţi Neo

4


...tî-ţeu en le-Iţi-to...

Shall salt if to Iţito!

5


...hue nî-heie iî-ţî uî-ine Iţi...

Is will be to salt and inside Iţi!

6


...nî uî-puiţ ene-Iţi ...

See and chik I'm Iţi!

7


... uî-i-şu ţe-Iţi şik min huţuot...

And to save salt Iţi shick from doors!

8


...Coehonin: Eghenon...

Priests: Aegeon!
